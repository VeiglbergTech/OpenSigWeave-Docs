## 2. Website delivery and GitHub Pages

This website consists of static files hosted by **GitHub Pages**, a service operated by GitHub, Inc., USA. When you visit, your browser exchanges technical information with GitHub infrastructure. This may include your IP address, request time and destination, browser and device information, and technical connection and error data.

GitHub states that **IP addresses of GitHub Pages visitors are logged and stored for security purposes**, whether or not visitors are signed in to GitHub.

The processing serves website delivery, security, availability and troubleshooting. For processing under our responsibility, **Article 6(1)(f) GDPR** may serve as the legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating functional and secure project documentation. Where GitHub acts as an independent controller, GitHub's own privacy information also applies.

The site operates **no separate visitor tracking system and no operator-controlled server-side access database**. We do not have direct access to GitHub's independently maintained security logs or control over their individual retention periods. Retention follows GitHub's applicable policies and security or deletion criteria. We cannot give a binding specific retention period for those logs.

Further information:

- [About GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages)
- [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## 4. On-site search and display preferences

The documentation uses **Starlight** and a **Pagefind search index** generated at website build time. Search requests are processed in the browser against static index files loaded from this website, without a separate external search service.

Starlight may store an explicitly selected light or dark display theme under **`starlight-theme`** in the browser's **localStorage**, allowing the visitor's preference to be restored. This setting serves only to display the chosen appearance. Without an explicitly saved choice, the system's display preference applies. You can delete this setting in your browser or select a different theme.

The current site configuration sets **no analytics or advertising cookies** and embeds no external tracking or marketing scripts. Local browser storage is used only for the stated display preference. If additional technology is introduced, any consent requirement under **Section 25 TDDDG** must be re-examined before deployment.

## 5. Contact by email

When you contact us by email, we process your sending address, message contents and, where applicable, technical transport information to answer and document the request.

For general enquiries, the legal basis is **Article 6(1)(f) GDPR** (handling and responding to the enquiry). Where pre-contractual measures or fulfilment of an agreement are concerned, **Article 6(1)(b) GDPR** may apply. Information is retained only for the period necessary to handle the enquiry and any relevant legal evidence or retention obligations. The participating email providers may also process technical connection information when transporting your message.

Providing an email address is voluntary, but without it an individual reply is not possible.

## 6. External links

This website links to third-party services, especially GitHub and the websites of documented software projects. Merely displaying a link does **not** automatically transmit data to its destination. If you open a link, the destination provider may process data under its own privacy notices.

Contact [info@veiglbergtech.com](mailto:info@veiglbergtech.com) to exercise your rights. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority (Article 77 GDPR). No solely automated decision-making within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR takes place on this documentation website.
