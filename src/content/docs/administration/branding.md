---
title: "Branding"
description: "Application name, accent color, logo and favicon"
---

The Branding area supports a customized application display name, primary accent color, PNG logo and PNG/ICO favicon. Settings are persisted and survive reload and container restart. Reset controls restore the shipped branding assets.

Upload validation and restrictions apply; do not assume that arbitrary SVG or executable content is accepted. Branding of the OpenSigWeave portal is separate from the Starlight documentation website's theme.

Check both light/dark readability after changing the accent. See the [implemented feature matrix](/reference/cli-api-v1/).
